Bonsai Support

For help with Bonsai Tree Intelligence, email macsonn141@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, what you were trying to do, and the error message or steps to reproduce it. Do not send passwords, payment details, private photos or a complete tree archive unless needed and you have reviewed what it contains.

Records and recovery. Bonsai keeps your collection on your device without a required Bonsai account. In Settings > Data, you can create a collection archive or choose an archive to inspect a recovery preview. Review the counts and conflicts before explicitly choosing merge or replace. Replacing records requires confirmation and a current export. Keep a usable independent copy before replacing a device, deleting the app or changing important records. An exported file and any device backup are separate copies; Bonsai does not operate a cloud backup service.

Bonsai Pro. Open Settings > Bonsai Pro for available plans, Restore Purchases and Manage Subscriptions. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Apple handles billing, renewal, cancellation and refund requests. If a purchase is pending or cannot be verified, do not assume Pro is active; check the displayed status and contact support if needed. Loss of Pro does not delete your existing records or prevent their basic export.

Care information. Bonsai helps you review recorded evidence, plans, actions and reported outcomes. Its attention and safety information is not a plant diagnosis or a guarantee of treatment results. If evidence is missing or a treatment label is unclear, defer the action and seek qualified local advice.

Privacy questions about Bonsai or information you voluntarily send to support can use the same email address.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
