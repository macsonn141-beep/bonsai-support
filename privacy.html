Bonsai Privacy Policy

This policy describes information handled by the Bonsai Tree Intelligence app and by voluntary email support.

Your local records. You choose the tree details, ownership and care events, observations, plans, actions, outcomes, notes, dates and photos you record. Bonsai derives attention lists, safety explanations, timelines, Tree Passports and visual comparisons from those records on your device. No Bonsai account is required. The app does not send your collection to a developer server or external AI service and contains no developer analytics, advertising tracker or Bonsai cloud sync.

Photos and preferences. You choose photos through the system photo picker. Imported photos can contain metadata, so review them before exporting or sharing. Language and timezone preferences are stored on the device. User-entered tree names and notes remain your original text when you change language.

Purchases. Apple's App Store handles purchase and payment processing under Apple's terms and privacy practices. Bonsai uses StoreKit's verified purchase and subscription status to determine Pro access. It does not ask for payment card information and does not send your purchase records to a developer-operated billing server. Connecting to the App Store may be necessary to retrieve products, purchase or restore access.

Exports, imports and other copies. An archive you create can contain tree records and photos. You decide whether to save or share it and with whom. Its destination and service provider may retain copies under their own practices. Importing an archive is an explicit action with validation and a preview before a chosen merge or replacement. Device or operating-system backup settings may also retain copies outside the app.

Managing information. Use the available in-app editing, export and deletion controls to manage local records. Deleting local information does not remove archives you previously exported, copies you shared or device-provider backups. Bonsai does not control those external copies.

Email support. If you email macsonn141@gmail.com, the email providers and recipient process the message and attachments you choose to send. This correspondence is separate from your local collection. Avoid sending sensitive or unnecessary information. You can contact the same address about information you supplied by email; no promise is made to erase copies held independently by other providers or recipients.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
