Bonsai Terms and Purchase Information

Licensed use of Bonsai Tree Intelligence is covered by the Apple Standard End User License Agreement shown for the app in the App Store. This page explains the product and its purchase options; it does not introduce a replacement custom EULA.

Purpose and care decisions. Bonsai is a personal tool for keeping bonsai history and reviewing evidence before care decisions. Its explanations are informational, not a diagnosis, a guarantee of plant health or a substitute for qualified local advice and treatment-product instructions. You choose what to record and which actions to take. Keep an independent copy of important records.

Free and Pro. Free supports up to 3 active trees, with existing records, essential safety warnings and basic export remaining available. Pro unlocks the same Pro feature set through any of three options: a monthly auto-renewable subscription, a yearly auto-renewable subscription, or a separate one-time Lifetime non-consumable purchase. Lifetime is not an auto-renewing subscription. The app explains the current Pro features and displays the actual App Store price and purchase terms before purchase.

The monthly plan has no introductory trial. Eligible Apple Accounts may receive 7 days free on the yearly plan, followed by the displayed yearly price. Eligibility and the offer shown by Apple govern whether a trial is available. Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. If you do not want a trial to renew, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Apple handles payment; manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account's subscription settings. Billing, access for the current period, cancellation and refunds follow Apple's applicable terms.

Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for purchase to restore eligible access. Pro access depends on verified purchase status, not merely a purchase attempt. Expiry or loss of Pro does not delete, automatically archive or truncate existing tree records and photos, and does not prevent their basic export. New active-tree creation or reactivation and Pro-only functionality remain subject to the displayed Free/Pro limits.

Your content and copies. Record only content you are entitled to use. Review archives before sharing or importing them. You choose their recipients and destinations; external copies are not controlled by Bonsai. Questions about app behavior or these product explanations can be sent to macsonn141@gmail.com.
